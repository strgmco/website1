  1. Stage 01

    ICP

    Defining who the product is actually for, narrowly enough to act on.

    Most early ICPs name a category, not a customer. "Mid-market fintech" doesn't tell you who has the problem, who feels it this quarter, or who can sign. We get to the specific person in the specific situation where your product is urgent rather than interesting — narrow enough that it's obvious who to approach, and obvious who to turn down.

  2. Stage 02

    Product-market fit

    Establishing whether the fit is real, and naming what would show it isn't.

    Enthusiasm isn't evidence, and neither is a waitlist. Before you go looking, we define what a real signal would have to look like — and what result would force you to change course. Then we go get it. Sometimes the answer is that the fit is there and you've been underselling it. Sometimes it isn't, and learning that in six weeks instead of six quarters is the whole return on the engagement.

  3. Stage 03

    Go-to-market

    How the first customers hear about you, and why they say yes.

    Before there's a funnel, there's a sequence of conversations that either work or they don't. We build the first repeatable version: the channel, the message, the qualification, the objection you'll hear every time and the answer to it. Not a strategy deck — a motion your team can run without you in the room.
